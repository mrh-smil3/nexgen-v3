# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Nexgen Operational Intelligence Landing Page

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft  
**Product:** Nexgen Corporate Landing Page  
**Primary Objective:** Reposition Nexgen from a conventional website development provider into a specialized digital technology partner focused on operational visibility, system integration, monitoring, and operational control.

---

# 1. Product Overview

## 1.1 Background

Nexgen initially positioned itself primarily as a website and digital development provider.

However, Nexgen has expanded its technical capabilities into more complex business applications, including:

- Enterprise system integration
- Data synchronization
- Operational monitoring
- Management information systems
- Business process applications
- Procurement monitoring
- Work Order monitoring
- Planning and budget absorption monitoring
- Integration with enterprise systems such as IBM Maximo
- Web application development
- IT infrastructure and support

One significant project is an operational monitoring platform for powerplant overhaul activities.

The system integrates with IBM Maximo and provides monitoring across:

- Work Orders
- Purchase Requisitions
- Purchase Orders
- Overhaul planning
- Procurement progress
- Budget absorption
- Operational status

This project demonstrates that Nexgen's capabilities extend beyond conventional website development.

The new landing page must communicate this evolution.

# 2. Strategic Positioning

## 2.1 Current Positioning

> Website Development & Digital Solutions

This positioning places Nexgen in a highly competitive market containing:

- Website agencies
- Freelance developers
- Software houses
- Digital agencies
- IT consultants

Price and feature comparison become major competitive factors.

---

## 2.2 Proposed Positioning

### Primary Positioning

> **Operational Intelligence & Control**

Nexgen helps organizations transform data from existing enterprise systems into operational visibility, monitoring, and actionable control.

### Supporting Positioning

> **The operational layer between enterprise systems and management decisions.**

Nexgen does not attempt to replace existing ERP, EAM, procurement, or enterprise systems.

Instead, Nexgen connects to existing systems and creates an operational layer that makes information easier to monitor, understand, and act upon.

# 5. Product Goals

## Primary Goals

1. Reposition Nexgen as an operational technology partner.
2. Differentiate Nexgen from conventional software houses.
3. Establish credibility through real enterprise project experience.
4. Generate qualified B2B leads.
5. Communicate Nexgen's ability to integrate with existing systems.
6. Demonstrate capability through case studies rather than generic service claims.

The landing page should NOT:

- Position Nexgen as an ERP replacement.
- Compete directly with IBM Maximo, SAP, Oracle, etc.

- Business applications
